Our Story

The Food Lover History

More than 25 years of experience

Food Lover began as a small family kitchen in the heart of Bosra Al-Sham, built on one simple belief: great food brings people together. For more than two decades we have welcomed guests to our table with generous plates and genuine hospitality.

Today our chefs blend time-honoured recipes with modern technique, sourcing the freshest local produce every morning so that each dish tells a story of flavour, craft and care.
